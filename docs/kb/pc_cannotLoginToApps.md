when a user comes in with complaint that they cannot login to apps, when those apps are associated for example, all are MS products, the cause is highly possible to be related with the service provider and its environment. On the other hand, if those apps seem unrelated, random, or general inability to login to most of the apps, highly possible causes include the below.
1. VPN connection error: the PC cannot connec to the trusted network for apps to use credentials of the user. Maybe just simply causing problem with inability to connect to the internet.
2. Network error: other than VPN, the PC's wifi card or wired connection and its pathway may be having some kind of issue and the PC is unable to connect to the internet.
3. Application broken: sometimes the app's image or data is broken or version is not up to date so it stops the users from using it. 

solutions 
If the cause may be due to the service provider issues, search for any global/non-local outage on the company portal or with your team or the service providers information/news.
For other causes, 1. check the VPN client app and see if there are any errors 2. check the wired and wireless connections, go to network settings, check the wifi card, try other network/wifi. 3. if you suspect the app is broken, open the app, compare the behaviour with a healthy app on another  device/account that has no apparent problem. You may need to check the logs and application settings within the app and on the device OS. Trying group update on the device may update the app to the company standard or check the company's application download protal to check for newer versions.

When having difficulty in targeting the cuase, you may want to consider logging the user into a different device and see the problem persists. This may be a walk around solution for the user to get back to his/her daily operations.
