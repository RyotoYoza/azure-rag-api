Printer health should be checked periodically. In our department, it is done every morning at least. 
Whenever you see an error or alert in the status column of the printer management portal, prompt investigation and problem solving is required, as it may cause widespread issue to the users using that printer.

Below is the basic flow of investigation.
1. check the details in the printer management portal. You may find some clue to the cuase. 
2. access the printer via browser using https://<printer hostname or IP address> , if you can not connect to it the printer may not be connected to the network and that may be causing the error status on the management portal. Or on the browser page, you may find further info on the cause of the issue. 
3. check the printer itself. go to the printer and see if there is anything wrong with it. there may be some alert on its control panel or you may find that the cables are not plugged in properly.
